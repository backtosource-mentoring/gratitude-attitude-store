Gratitude Attitude

A Practical Guide to Consciousness Transformation

Are you living at your full potential, or are your plans still sitting next to your doubts? Your mind is your most powerful tool. It can carry you toward what matters, or it can quietly hold you in place.

In Gratitude Attitude, Esther Michele Kazir lays out a clear, practical route from survival mode to a life of purpose, abundance and joy. You follow Lily, a determined character who refuses to settle for her limits, and see each principle at work in a real life before you try it in yours.

What is the book about?

It is a practical guide to shifting from survival mode into intention. Through the story of Lily, a determined character who refuses to accept her limits, the book walks through the principles of how attention and gratitude shape the life you build.

Is there a Kindle edition?

Yes. Gratitude Attitude is available on Amazon as a Kindle eBook and as a hardcover.

Is there a workbook to go with it?

Yes. The Gratitude Attitude Workbook turns the ideas into daily exercises. It comes in eleven hardcover cover editions with the same interior.
