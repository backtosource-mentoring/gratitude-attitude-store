The author

Esther Michele Kazir

Author of the Gratitude Attitude collection.

Esther writes for people who have read enough about gratitude and are ready to practice it. Her books take ideas she has lived with for years, from the teachings of Abraham Hicks, Bruce Lipton and Viktor Frankl, and turn them into simple tools: a few lines in the morning, a signature at night, a page that waits for evidence.

The original book, Gratitude Attitude, follows Lily from survival mode to intention. The workbook turns each idea into exercises. The Agreement Journal runs a 42-day cycle of gratitude written in the past tense.

"The sky has woven stars for you, all that remains is to agree."
