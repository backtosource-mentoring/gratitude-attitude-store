The method

Five minutes. Three moves.

Every book in the collection runs on the same daily loop. Simple enough to keep, specific enough to work.

01

Notice

Catch what is already working before the mind lists what is missing. Three things, written down, specific.

02

Agree

Write what you want in the past tense, as if it already arrived. Sign it. Wishing becomes a decision.

03

Act

One aligned action today. Small is fine. Then log the evidence when it shows up, with the date.

Questions

Before you choose

What is Gratitude Attitude?

Gratitude Attitude is a collection of books by Esther Michele Kazir: the original book, a hands-on workbook and a 42-day journal. Together they turn gratitude from a feeling you wait for into a short daily practice you run on purpose.

Is every workbook cover different inside?

No. All eleven covers share the same 97-page interior. The cover is the only difference, so you can pick the one that fits you, or the person you are gifting.

Where can I buy the books?

Every edition is sold and shipped by Amazon. Each button on this site opens the exact Amazon page for that edition.

Which one should I start with?

Start with the book if you want the ideas and the story of Lily. Start with the workbook if you want to practice from day one. Choose The Agreement Journal if you already practice and want a 42-day record of what arrived.

Who are these books for?

For every soul ready to move from autopilot to intention. Entrepreneurs, parents, students, people starting over. No experience needed.

Are the books hardcover?

Yes. The workbook and The Agreement Journal are premium hardcovers. The original book is available in hardcover and as a Kindle eBook.

Your practice starts tonight.

Pick a book. Open it before bed. Write three lines. That is the whole beginning.
