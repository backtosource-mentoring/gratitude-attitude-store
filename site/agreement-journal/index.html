The Agreement Journal

Signed & Arrived: 42 Evenings of Gratitude and Evidence

You have written down what you want before. You have said the affirmations. What you have never had is proof.

This journal runs one 42-day cycle on a single idea: write it in the past tense, sign your name to it, and leave the facing page empty until reality catches up. At the end you are not holding a book of hopes. You are holding a dated record of what your own writing came before.

How each evening works

  • Left page, the agreement: one quote, three lines of gratitude for something not yet here, one aligned action, your signature
  • Right page, the evidence: blank until something arrives, then what came and the date
  • A weekly opener and a weekly reset
  • Undated. Start tonight, repeat the cycle as often as you need

Sign it. Log it. Prove it.

Is The Agreement Journal dated?

No. It is undated. Start any evening and repeat the 42-day cycle as many times as you like.

How long does each evening take?

About five to ten minutes: one quote, three lines of gratitude written in the past tense, one aligned action, and your signature.

What goes on the evidence page?

It stays blank until something arrives. Then you write what came and the date it came. Over 42 days you build a dated record.

How is it different from the workbook?

The workbook teaches the practice with exercises and frameworks. The journal is a focused 42-day cycle for logging agreements and evidence.
